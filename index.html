Who's behind STEEP map

From the community, for the community

We're a small group of developers and committed skiers. We started STEEP map because we wanted a better way to connect skiers all around the world and share what we love most - the experience of the backcountry.

Unlike other platforms, we believe the knowledge the community shares should stay accessible to everyone, free of charge.

Built by skiers

We ride the same lines we map. Every feature comes from what we need to plan the next day out.

The guidebook is free

Every tour, track and note the community shares is there for everyone to see - the shared knowledge should never cost a thing.

Rewarding our supporters

Those who choose to back us with Pro unlock exciting extras - and their support keeps new features coming for free and Pro users alike.

We're just getting started - and we're committed to keeping the mountain open for everyone.
