About this tour

A splendid and solitary orographic hike in the wild Val Vedello. From the settlement of Agneda, after a flat stretch you ascend in a winding manner along the unsealed road leading to the dam of Lago di Scais. You cross the lake keeping the right side and arrive at the mouth of Val Vedello. You pass the ruins of an old mine and finally turn left to climb the steep valley between Piz Cavrin and Cima Soliva. Here you find a long and satisfying slope with sustained gradients, which, if you wish to continue, allows you to reach Cima Soliva via the final chute.
