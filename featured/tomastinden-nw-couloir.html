About this tour

A spectacular ski-mountaineering outing in the Lakselvtindane range of the Lyngen Alps. The tour starts near Lakselvbukt and climbs steadily towards the impressive Tomasrenna, a long NW-facing couloir leading to the high glacial plateau known as the Himmelske Fredsplass (“Place of Heavenly Peace”). From the plateau, the route continues towards Tomastinden, following steep terrain and a short exposed section to reach the summit ridge.

The descent offers a rewarding combination of steep couloir skiing, glacier terrain and more mellow lower slopes. The route is north to northwest facing and can hold good snow, although conditions in the couloir and on the upper slopes can vary considerably. Expect a long day with around 1,500 m of vertical gain, steep snow terrain and sections where crampons and an ice axe may be useful.

A beautiful and committing line combining classic Lyngen scenery with technical ski-mountaineering terrain.
